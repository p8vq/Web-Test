Gaming

     Gaming is my favorite hobby, mostly because it is something I have grew up with and have enjoyed ever since then. Having been born in 2008, as a child my family had a Nintendo Wii. Being a gift for my brother from my parents, he played with it every day. I was not old enough to play video games at the time, but something my parents did was give me an empty Wii Remote and let me pretend I was playing with my brother. I have photos of this dated back to as early as 2009, and it was the start of something special and integral to me.

    Upon reaching the age of about 2-3, I was finally able to start playing the Wii myself, with my parents buying kids games for me. While I do not have the copies of the first two games I ever played, I do remember both of their names, with one being a Sesame Street educational game, and the other being a Ni hao, Kai-Lan licensed mini-game collection. These were some of my first experiences with video games, and they were perfect for me due to the simplicity of the Wii Remote on its side and easy AR aiming when using it horizontally. It was the perfect console for educational titles, and it allowed me to get more and more used to playing video games themselves. After I started getting more used to gaming in general, I was able to move from Early Childhood-rated games to E for Everyone-rated games. I remember the first non-educational game I ever played was SpongeBob's Atlantis SquarePantis and I remember it quite fondly, mostly becuase I still own a copy of the disc and remember when in 2012 I brought it to my family in Californa, only to realise they did not have a Wii. I also played a lot of Mario Kart Wii with my brother, and it was the game we played the most together overall. We also frequently played Wii Sports Resort along with other games that were in our library. When I got older, the variety of games I played got larger and larger. It's impossible for me to list every single one, but I frequently played almost all of the Wii series games (Wii Sports, Wii Sports Resort, Wii Play, Wii Fit (Plus), and Wii Music), New Super Mario Bros. Wii, Kirby's Epic Yarn, Rayman Raving Rabbids, Carnival Games and Mario Party 8.

    Along with this, the Wii was not the only game console my family had at home. In about 2010-11, my grandparents had gotten my brother yet another console as gift, that being the Xbox 360. It was around the time of the release of the Kinect, which made my introduction to the Xbox 360 a lot easier due to the ease of use of the Kinect, in which you didn't even need a controller to turn it on. We had about 3 games for the Kinect at first, those being Kinect Adventures, Kinect Sports and later, Kinect Sports Season Two. I was completely memorised by the technology at the time at such a young age. Being able to simply move around without anything in your hands while also being able to play with my brother at the same time was something magical. While I did overall play the Wii more than the Xbox 360, I ultimately believe that my fondness for video games would not be the same without the Xbox 360. The Xbox 360 was also the first time I was able to sense traditional video gaming, as a few other games we had for the console were non-Kinect titles. I was used to the simple Wii Remote and Nunchuk, but for the most part I was able to get used to a traditional video game controller such as the Xbox 360 controller. I had LEGO Batman, Zuma, Minecraft, Xbox Live Arcade and Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts & Bolts.
